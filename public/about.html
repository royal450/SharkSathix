About SharkSathix

SharkSathix koi bank, NBFC ya finance company nahi hai. Ye Sarkvission ka help aur education platform hai jahan online earning, credit card, loan, demat account aur online safety ko simple Hindi me step-by-step samjhaya jata hai, taaki log sahi product choose karke apply ya account open kar sakein.

Content kaise banta hai

  • Har guide general education aur practical help ke liye hai.
  • Kuch pages par referral ya affiliate links ho sakte hain. Agar aap un links se product lete hain, account kholte hain ya apply karte hain, to SharkSathix ko partner se commission mil sakta hai.
  • Commission milne ka matlab ye nahi ki product har kisi ke liye sahi hai; eligibility, charges aur approval partner ke rules par depend karte hain.
  • Rules aur charges samay ke saath badalte hain, isliye har guide me official source check karne ki salah di jati hai.
  • Guide me galti dikhe to Contact page se correction bheja ja sakta hai.

Education only

SharkSathix personal financial advice nahi deta, loan ya card approval ki guarantee nahi deta, aur investment recommendation nahi karta. Koi bhi loan, card ya investment decision lene se pehle bank/NBFC/broker ki official terms khud check karein.
