Credit Card Guide

Credit card useful tool hai, lekin late payment par interest bahut mehenga pad sakta hai.

Safe use ke rules

  • Har month full bill time par pay karein.
  • Minimum due ko full payment samajhne ki galti na karein.
  • Card limit ka zyada use credit score ko hurt kar sakta hai.
  • Annual fee, joining fee aur reward conditions pehle padhein.

Credit score

Time par payment aur kam utilization credit profile ke liye achha hota hai. Score improve hone me time lagta hai, koi shortcut guarantee nahi hoti.

Official sources

  • Reserve Bank of India (RBI) — credit card rules aur consumer protection ke liye official source.
  • Apne card issuer ki official website par bill, interest aur charges ki latest terms check karein.
