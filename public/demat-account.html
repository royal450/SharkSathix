Demat Account Guide

Demat account shares aur securities ko electronic form me rakhne ke liye use hota hai.

Account kholne se pehle

  • PAN, Aadhaar, bank account aur nominee details ready rakhein.
  • Broker ka registration aur charges official website par check karein.
  • Account opening, annual maintenance, brokerage aur DP charges samjhein.

Safety rules

  • OTP, password ya PIN kisi ke saath share na karein.
  • Guaranteed return ka promise karne walo se door rahein.
  • Investment se pehle risk samjhein; market me loss bhi ho sakta hai.

Ye guide education ke liye hai, investment advice nahi. Apna decision apni research aur risk capacity se lein.

Official sources

  • SEBI — securities market ke rules aur registered intermediaries ki jaankari ke liye official source.
  • Apne broker aur depository (CDSL/NSDL) ki official website par charges aur account terms check karein.
