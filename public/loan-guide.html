Loan Guide

Loan lene se pehle sirf approval nahi, total cost samajhna sabse important hai.

Loan se pehle checklist

  • EMI aapki monthly income me comfortably fit hoti hai ya nahi.
  • Interest rate ke saath processing fee, GST aur late fee check karein.
  • Loan amount utna hi lein jitna zaroori ho.
  • Prepayment ya foreclosure charge hai ya nahi, pehle pooch lein.

Common documents

Aadhaar, PAN, address proof, bank statement aur income proof aksar manga jata hai. Final list bank/NBFC par depend karti hai.

Common mistakes

  • Sirf EMI dekhna, total repayment na dekhna.
  • Fake loan apps ya advance fee mangne walo par bharosa karna.
  • Ek saath kai jagah apply karke credit profile kharab karna.

SharkSathix loan approval ki guarantee nahi deta. Hamesha official bank/NBFC channel se apply karein.

Official sources

  • Reserve Bank of India (RBI) — banking aur NBFC rules ke liye official source.
  • Apne bank/NBFC ki official website par loan ki latest terms, fees aur charges check karein.
