Tech Safety Guide

Online fraud aksar urgency aur lalach se hota hai. Ruk kar verify karna sabse bada safety step hai.

Basic safety

  • OTP, PIN aur password kabhi share na karein.
  • Unknown link par bank/UPI details na bharein.
  • Payment receive karne ke liye PIN nahi lagta; PIN sirf payment bhejne ke liye hota hai.
  • Important accounts par two-factor authentication on rakhein.

Fraud ho jaye to

Turant bank ko inform karein, card/UPI block karein aur official cybercrime reporting channel use karein.

Official sources

  • NPCI — UPI se judi official jaankari ke liye.
  • National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal — online fraud report karne ke liye official portal.
